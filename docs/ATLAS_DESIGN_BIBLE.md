# ATLAS DESIGN BIBLE

Version: 1.0.0

Project: TrustVault

Last Updated: 27 July 2026

Status: Active

---

# Purpose

The Atlas Design Bible defines the official visual identity, behaviour, personality, illustration standards and usage rules for Atlas, the TrustVault mascot.

This document exists to ensure Atlas remains visually and behaviourally consistent across every TrustVault experience, including:

- Website
- Marketplace
- Checkout
- Gift Vault
- Seller Storefront
- Dashboard
- Mobile Applications
- Documentation
- Marketing
- Social Media
- Future Products

Every future illustration, animation, icon or AI-generated asset must follow the standards defined in this document.

---

# Vision

Atlas is more than a mascot.

Atlas is the visual embodiment of TrustVault.

Whenever users see Atlas, they should immediately associate him with:

• Trust

• Security

• Reliability

• Professionalism

• Protection

Atlas exists to reassure users throughout their journey.

Atlas should never distract from the product.

Instead, Atlas quietly reinforces confidence at every important interaction.

---

# Brand Relationship

## TrustVault

Atlas represents TrustVault.

Atlas is the official TrustVault mascot.

Atlas should always reinforce TrustVault's values.

TrustVault is always the primary brand.

---

## Arc

TrustVault is built on Arc.

Arc provides the infrastructure.

Atlas does NOT represent Arc.

Whenever Arc is referenced:

Always write:

Arc

Never write:

arc

Never write:

ARC

Approved wording:

Built on Arc

This attribution should remain subtle and secondary to the TrustVault brand.

The Arc logo must never be modified.

The Arc logo must never become part of Atlas.

TrustVault branding must always take priority.

# Illustration Philosophy

Atlas should support the interface.

Atlas should never dominate the interface.

Illustrations should enhance trust rather than distract users.

Whitespace is preferred over decoration.

Every illustration should have a clear purpose.

---

# Accessibility

Atlas illustrations must support accessibility.

Avoid flashing elements.

Avoid excessive animation.

Maintain sufficient colour contrast.

Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.

Always support reduced-motion preferences.

---

# Technical Standards

Preferred formats:

SVG

PNG

WebP

Optimise all assets.

Keep file sizes as small as possible.

Use transparent backgrounds whenever practical.

Provide descriptive filenames.
