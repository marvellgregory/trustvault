# TrustVault Brand Asset Registry

## Usage rules
- TrustVault remains the primary product brand.
- Use Arc only with supported language such as “Built on Arc”, “Available on Arc”, or “Supports Arc”.
- Never merge Arc into the TrustVault wordmark or product name.
- Do not recolor, redraw, crop, distort, rotate, or add effects to official Arc or Circle assets.
- Preserve required clear space around every official logo.
- Arc’s digital minimum is 50 px in height.
- Circle’s full wordmark digital minimum is 120 px in width.
- Use official USDC artwork only after it is added from the official Circle brand kit.
- Do not represent Circle or Arc endorsement, certification, or partnership unless formally authorized.

## Included
- TrustVault logo, wordmark, and icon.
- Official Arc assets copied unchanged from the supplied Arc package.
- Official Circle assets copied unchanged from the supplied Circle package.

## Not included yet
- USDC logo/token artwork was not present in the uploaded Circle ZIP.
- EURC and USYC assets were not present.
- Atlas mascot files were not present in the uploaded branding ZIP.

## TrustVault SVG note
The supplied TrustVault SVG files do not include viewBox attributes. They should be optimized before production use so they scale reliably in responsive layouts.
